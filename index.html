Eye ointment

“Squeeze out ointment and you can't tell where it landed.”

Your lids and lashes fill the screen, so you can see what you're doing. No add-ons — it works with any bottle, tube or lens.

怎么用HOW TO USE

举起手机，三步就好

Hold it up — three steps

  1. 把手机举在眼前 20–40 厘米，屏幕对着自己。滴眼药水、涂眼膏时可以仰头，把手机举在脸的正上方。

    Hold your iPhone 20–40 cm (8–16 in) from your face, screen toward you. For eye drops or ointment, tilt your head back and hold the phone above you.

  2. 屏幕下方的放大窗会自动锁住一只眼睛并放大显示。

    The magnifier at the bottom finds one eye and locks onto it.

  3. 想看另一只眼：按住放大窗里的画面拖向那只眼，松手就换过去。

    To switch eyes, press the image in the magnifier, drag it toward the other eye and let go.

横拿时把摄像头放在左边，锁得更稳。换眼、暂时跟丢等细节见使用说明。

Holding it sideways? Keep the camera on the left for a steadier lock. More details in the guide.
