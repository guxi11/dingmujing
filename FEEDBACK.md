# 反馈指引

取景不准、机型不兼容、功能建议，或任何想说的，都欢迎反馈。

## 两种方式

- **GitHub Issues（推荐）**：<https://github.com/Guxi11/dingmujing/issues> —— 问题与处理进展公开可查。
- **邮件**：2364194350@qq.com —— 在 App 光板页脚点「反馈 → 写邮件反馈」，正文会自动带上机型与系统版本，可随意删改。

## 怎样写更容易被修好

1. 机型（如 iPhone 15 Pro）与 iOS 版本；
2. App 版本（「反馈」页底部可见）；
3. 持机方式：竖拿，还是横拿、摄像头在左边还是右边；手机离脸大概多远；
4. 现象：发生了什么、你期望的是什么、能否稳定复现。

**请不要在 Issues 里上传带有你面部的照片或录屏** —— Issues 是公开的。确有需要请改用邮件。

隐私相关说明见 [隐私政策](PRIVACY.md)。

# Feedback

Framing problems, device compatibility, feature ideas, or anything else — all welcome.

## Two ways to reach us

- **GitHub Issues (preferred)**: <https://github.com/Guxi11/dingmujing/issues> — issues and progress are public.
- **Email**: 2364194350@qq.com — in the app, tap "Feedback → Email Feedback" in the footer; your device model and iOS version are filled in automatically and can be edited.

## What helps us fix it

1. Device model (e.g. iPhone 15 Pro) and iOS version;
2. App version (shown at the bottom of the Feedback page);
3. How you hold it: upright, or sideways with the camera on the left or right; roughly how far from your face;
4. What happened, what you expected, and whether it reproduces.

**Please don't upload photos or recordings of your face to Issues** — they are public. Use email if you need to share one.

See the [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY.md#english).
