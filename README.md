# 定目镜 · DropSight

iOS 上的滴眼药水辅助取景工具：仰头时用前置摄像头把一只眼睛放大到屏幕上，并从眼心立起一支始终指向正上方的箭头，提示药瓶该从哪一侧送进来。

- [反馈指引](FEEDBACK.md) · [提交 Issue](https://github.com/Guxi11/dingmujing/issues)

## 使用说明

- **屏幕就是灯**：仰头，把手机举在脸上方 20–40 厘米，屏幕对着眼睛。屏幕会调到最亮给眼睛补光；在白色区域上下滑动可调暗或调亮。
- **放大窗**：下方的窗口放大显示一只眼睛。认出虹膜时，所选贴图会印在虹膜上。
- **箭头**：从眼睛立起的箭头始终指向正上方，提示药瓶从哪一侧送进来。它只指方向，不判断能不能滴。
- **换眼**：按住放大窗里的画面拖向另一只眼，松手就换过去；没拖过半程就留在原来那只。
- **暂时跟丢**：眨眼或被遮住时画面会先定住，放大窗的一角收成小方角并亮起一个点；眼睛重新露出来会自动锁回。遮住当前这只眼约 3 秒，若另一只眼看得清，通常会自动换过去。
- **横拿**：横拿时把摄像头放在左边：这样脸在摄像头里是正的，跟踪更稳。

系统要求：iOS 17 及以上的 iPhone。带原深感（TrueDepth）摄像头的机型跟踪更稳；无原深感的机型（如 iPhone SE）也可使用。

定目镜只提供取景与方向提示，不是医疗器械，不提供医疗建议；用药请遵医嘱。

## DropSight (English)

An eye-drop aiming aid for iPhone. With your head tilted back, the front camera magnifies one eye on screen and raises an arrow from it that always points straight up, showing which side to bring the bottle in from.

- [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY.md#english)
- [Feedback](FEEDBACK.md#english) · [Open an issue](https://github.com/Guxi11/dingmujing/issues)

**How to use**

- **The screen is your light** — Tilt your head back and hold the phone 20–40 cm above your face, screen toward your eyes. The screen goes to full brightness to light your eye; swipe up or down on the white area to dim or brighten it.
- **Magnifier** — The window at the bottom shows one eye up close. When the iris is recognized, your chosen sticker appears on it.
- **Arrow** — The arrow rising from the eye always points straight up. It only shows direction — it doesn't judge whether you're ready to drop.
- **Switch eyes** — Press and drag the image in the magnifier toward the other eye and let go to switch. Release before halfway to stay on the current eye.
- **Lost for a moment** — When you blink or something covers the eye, the image holds still, one corner of the magnifier turns square and a dot lights up; it locks back on by itself once the eye is visible again. If the current eye stays covered for about 3 seconds and the other eye is clearly visible, it usually switches over automatically.
- **Holding it sideways** — Keep the camera on the left: your face is then upright to the camera, so tracking is steadier.

Requires an iPhone with iOS 17 or later. Tracking is steadier on models with a TrueDepth camera.

DropSight is an aiming aid only. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice.
